Data, assumptions and limits
Sources
NESO Enduring Auction Capability results (frequency response, reserve); Elexon Insights bid-offer acceptance data (Balancing Mechanism).
Revenue method
£/MW/day = Balancing Mechanism revenue over the window ÷ registered capacity ÷ days in window. EAC clearing price and volume shown separately, not summed into this figure.
Participant scope
Battery storage units only. Co-located or hybrid sites shown at their registered BMU capacity.
