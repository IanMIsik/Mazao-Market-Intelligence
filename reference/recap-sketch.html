What moved the week

Three drivers, each with the number that matters and what it meant for dispatch.

Wind output

4.8 GW daily low on Wednesday, against 14.8 GW on Saturday. The midweek lull is the whole story for prices.

Peak demand

31.2 GW peak on Friday. Demand was unremarkable, so the price move came from supply rather than load.

Periods above £100

29 settlement periods cleared above £100 on Wednesday, against 0 on Monday. Tight margin, not a system failure.

For a battery, the week rewarded patience rather than volume. Value concentrated in the 17:00 to 20:00 window on three days, and cycling outside that window mostly gave back the spread. The same pattern held in July.
