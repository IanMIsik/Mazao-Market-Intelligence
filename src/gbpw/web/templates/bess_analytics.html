Data, assumptions and limits
Sources
NESO Enduring Auction Capability results (frequency response, reserve); Elexon EBOCF (Balancing Mechanism cashflow).
Revenue method
Total BM revenue and £/MW/day count offer cashflow only — sum of every matched battery unit's accepted-offer cashflow over the window, ÷ registered capacity ÷ days for the £/MW/day figure, shown only where capacity is known and nonzero. An accepted offer is a unit instructed to increase output (or reduce demand): genuine incremental BM revenue. An accepted bid is a unit instructed to decrease output (or increase demand) — a payment the unit makes to National Grid, not revenue, since that volume was normally already sold elsewhere (e.g. day-ahead). Bid cashflow is shown separately (see "Offer / bid revenue" above) for transparency but excluded from these two figures. EAC clearing volume/price shown separately, not summed into this figure.
Accepted volumes
Not yet available for the Balancing Mechanism section — EBOCF gives cashflow only. Needs a separate ISPSTACK ingest.
Participant scope
Battery storage units only (NESO technologyType = Batteries), joined to Elexon BM units via the shared national grid BMU code.
Status
Live data. Balancing Mechanism accepted-volume figures are not yet available — see above.
