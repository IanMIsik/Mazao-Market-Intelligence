Methodology & attribution

Hover a slice to see its own CI factor and how it's calculated. Figures for imports are separated by country where NESO publishes a distinct factor (France, Netherlands, Ireland) — CI varies a lot by source grid, e.g. France is mainly nuclear while Irish imports have a relatively high proportion of fossil fuels in the mix. Every other import country is grouped as "Other imports" using the generic factor, since NESO doesn't publish a country-specific one for those links. Battery and Pumped Storage are treated as zero CI at point of generation. Data updates every 5 minutes. See NESO's Carbon Intensity site for a list of CIs used and to search historical CI data going back to 2009.
